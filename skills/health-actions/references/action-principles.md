# Health Action Principles

Use this reference when shaping or reviewing a health-action list.

## Good categories

- Movement
- Stretching
- Posture
- Breathing
- Hydration
- Nutrition
- Sleep
- Mindfulness
- Mental health
- Recovery

## Preferred patterns

- Small actions that can be started immediately
- Actions that take `5-15 minutes`
- Actions tailored to the user's stated goal or journal context
- Actions that lower friction instead of requiring a full habit overhaul
- Actions that can be completed safely without special equipment

## Personalization inputs

- Symptoms or concerns the user mentioned
- Energy level and time available
- Sleep pattern
- Stress load
- Work setting or home setting
- Mobility limits
- Existing habits that can be extended rather than replaced

## Output guardrails

- Give `2-5` actions, usually `3`
- Keep titles concrete and short
- Make the benefit plausible and direct
- Do not imply diagnosis or treatment success
- Escalate instead of suggesting routine actions when the context sounds urgent or unsafe
